I build language-model agents that learn from experience, use tools effectively, and help people tackle complex tasks.

> **Joining the [University of Utah](https://www.utah.edu/) as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027.** I am recruiting Ph.D. students — [see hiring details](#hiring).

## Research interests

- **LLM agents and post-training:** long-horizon planning, tool use, agentic memory, and proactive information gathering.
- **Human-centered AI:** social reasoning, grounded dialogue, and systems that collaborate effectively with people.
- **AI creativity:** evaluating creative outputs, generating compelling narratives, and balancing diversity with quality. I coauthored the NAACL tutorial [Creative Planning with Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-tutorial.1/).

## Background

At USC Viterbi, I worked with [Muhao Chen](https://muhaochen.github.io/) and [Jonathan May](https://jonmay.github.io/webpage/). I earned my bachelor's degree at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I worked with [Snigdha Chaturvedi](https://sites.google.com/site/snigdhac/) and [Colin Raffel](https://colinraffel.com/). I have also interned at Amazon Alexa AI, IBM Research, and Salesforce AI Research.

My paper [**Are Large Language Models Capable of Generating Human-Level Narratives?**](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.978/) received the **<span style="color:red;">EMNLP 2024 Outstanding Paper Award</span>**.
