I am Tenghao Huang, a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft's [Office of Applied Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/office-of-applied-research/). My research focuses on **post-training large language models, LLM agents, and human-centered AI**. I work toward agents that can learn from experience, reason about people and their goals, and help solve real-world problems.

**I will join the [University of Utah](https://www.utah.edu/) as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027!** I am recruiting Ph.D. students to join my lab. Please see the [Hiring](#hiring) section below for details.

My current interests include **long-horizon planning and tool use**, **agentic memory and proactive information gathering**, and **social reasoning and human-AI collaboration**. Recent work includes [GTA](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.856/), which generates long-horizon tasks for web agents, and [GLARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12165), which studies adversarial reward learning for forecasting social dynamics.

I also study **AI creativity**, including how to evaluate creative behavior, balance diversity and quality, and help people compose richer narratives. My recent work explores [long-tail creativity evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12998) and [base-aligned model collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05650). Check out our NAACL 2025 tutorial on [Creative Planning with Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-tutorial.1/)!

At USC Viterbi School of Engineering, I worked with Prof. [Muhao Chen](https://muhaochen.github.io/) and Prof. [Jonathan May](https://jonmay.github.io/webpage/). Before USC, I received my bachelor's degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. [Snigdha Chaturvedi](https://sites.google.com/site/snigdhac/) and Prof. [Colin Raffel](https://colinraffel.com/). I have interned at Amazon Alexa AI, IBM Research, Microsoft's Office of Applied Research, and Salesforce AI Research.

Our paper [**Are Large Language Models Capable of Generating Human-Level Narratives?**](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.978/) received the **<span style="color:red;">Outstanding Paper Award 🏆</span>** at EMNLP 2024!

## Hiring

I am looking for motivated Ph.D. students to join my lab at the University of Utah starting in **Fall 2027**. If you are interested in LLM post-training, agents, human-centered AI, or creativity, I would love to hear from you.

**Prospective Ph.D. students:** Please apply through the [Ph.D. application process](https://www.cs.utah.edu/graduate/prospective-students/admissions/graduate-application/) at the University of Utah's Kahlert School of Computing and **list me as a potential advisor** in your application so I can review your materials.

To reach out, email [ohara.lab.recruiting@gmail.com](mailto:ohara.lab.recruiting@gmail.com) with the subject **[PRSP PhD] + your current position and institution/organization**. A short email explaining your research interests and a concrete motivation for joining the lab is a great way to start a conversation. I will try to read all messages, but I may not be able to reply to every email.
