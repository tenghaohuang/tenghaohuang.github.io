I build language-model agents that solve open-ended and human-centered problems through learning approaches.

> **Joining the [University of Utah](https://www.utah.edu/) as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027.** I am recruiting Ph.D. students — [see hiring details](#hiring).

## Research interests

- **LLM agents and post-training:** long-horizon planning and tool use<sup><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.856/" title="GTA: Generating Long-Horizon Tasks for Web Agents at Scale" aria-label="Related paper 1: GTA: Generating Long-Horizon Tasks for Web Agents at Scale">[1]</a></sup>, agentic memory<sup><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12485" title="R2D2: Remembering, Replaying and Dynamic Decision Making with a Reflective Agentic Memory" aria-label="Related paper 2: R2D2: Remembering, Replaying and Dynamic Decision Making with a Reflective Agentic Memory">[2]</a></sup>, and proactive information gathering<sup><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21389" title="Teaching Language Models to Gather Information Proactively" aria-label="Related paper 3: Teaching Language Models to Gather Information Proactively">[3]</a></sup>.
- **Human-centered AI:** social reinforcement learning<sup><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12165" title="GLARE: Generative Learning via Adversarial Reward Estimation For Social Dynamics Forecasting" aria-label="Related paper 4: GLARE: Generative Learning via Adversarial Reward Estimation For Social Dynamics Forecasting">[4]</a></sup>, natural language understanding, and systems that collaborate effectively with people<sup><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1580/" title="NewsInterview: a Dataset and a Playground to Evaluate LLMs' Grounding Gap via Informational Interviews" aria-label="Related paper 5: NewsInterview: a Dataset and a Playground to Evaluate LLMs' Grounding Gap via Informational Interviews">[5]</a></sup>.
- **AI creativity:** theories of AI creativity<sup><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12998" title="Evaluating LLM Creativity through Long-Tail Performance" aria-label="Related paper 6: Evaluating LLM Creativity through Long-Tail Performance">[6]</a></sup>, designing AI-assisted writing systems<sup><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-tutorial.1/" title="Creative Planning with Language Models: Practice, Evaluation and Applications" aria-label="Related work 7: Creative Planning with Language Models: Practice, Evaluation and Applications">[7]</a></sup>, and balancing diversity with quality<sup><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05650" title="Optimizing Diversity and Quality through Base-Aligned Model Collaboration" aria-label="Related paper 8: Optimizing Diversity and Quality through Base-Aligned Model Collaboration">[8]</a></sup>.

## Background

At USC Viterbi, I worked with [Muhao Chen](https://muhaochen.github.io/) and [Jonathan May](https://jonmay.github.io/webpage/). I earned my bachelor's degree at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I worked with [Snigdha Chaturvedi](https://sites.google.com/site/snigdhac/) and [Colin Raffel](https://colinraffel.com/). I have also interned at Amazon Alexa AI, IBM Research, and Salesforce AI Research. My research has been reported by MIT Technology Review, and I received the **<span style="color:red;">EMNLP 2024 Outstanding Paper Award</span>**.
