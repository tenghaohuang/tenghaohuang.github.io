I am Tenghao Huang, a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft’s Office of Applied Research. My research focuses on post-training large language models, LLM agents, and human-centered AI. I work toward agents that can learn from experience, reason about people and their goals, and help solve real-world problems.

I will join the University of Utah as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027! I am recruiting Ph.D. students to join my lab. Please see the Hiring section below for details.

My current interests include long-horizon planning and tool use, agentic memory and proactive information gathering, and social reasoning and human-AI collaboration. Recent work includes GTA, which generates long-horizon tasks for web agents, and GLARE, which studies adversarial reward learning for forecasting social dynamics.

I also study AI creativity, including how to evaluate creative behavior, balance diversity and quality, and help people compose richer narratives. My recent work explores long-tail creativity evaluation and base-aligned model collaboration. Check out our NAACL 2025 tutorial on Creative Planning with Language Models!

At USC Viterbi School of Engineering, I worked with Prof. Muhao Chen and Prof. Jonathan May. Before USC, I received my bachelor’s degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Snigdha Chaturvedi and Prof. Colin Raffel. I have interned at Amazon Alexa AI, IBM Research, Microsoft’s Office of Applied Research, and Salesforce AI Research.

Our paper Are Large Language Models Capable of Generating Human-Level Narratives? received the Outstanding Paper Award 🏆 at EMNLP 2024!

Hiring

I am looking for motivated Ph.D. students to join my lab at the University of Utah starting in Fall 2027. If you are interested in LLM post-training, agents, human-centered AI, or creativity, I would love to hear from you.

Prospective Ph.D. students: Please apply through the Ph.D. application process at the University of Utah’s Kahlert School of Computing and list me as a potential advisor in your application so I can review your materials. To reach out, email ohara.lab.recruiting@gmail.com with the subject [PRSP PhD] + your current position and institution/organization. A short email explaining your research interests and a concrete motivation for joining the lab is a great way to start a conversation. I will try to read all messages, but I may not be able to reply to every email.
