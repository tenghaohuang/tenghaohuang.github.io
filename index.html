Tenghao Huang

Senior Applied Scientist, Office of Applied Research, Microsoft.

I build language-model agents that learn from experience, use tools effectively, and help people tackle complex tasks.

Joining the University of Utah as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027. I am recruiting Ph.D. students — see hiring details.

Research interests

  • LLM agents and post-training: long-horizon planning, tool use, agentic memory, and proactive information gathering.
  • Human-centered AI: social reasoning, grounded dialogue, and systems that collaborate effectively with people.
  • AI creativity: evaluating creative outputs, generating compelling narratives, and balancing diversity with quality. I coauthored the NAACL tutorial Creative Planning with Language Models.

Background

At USC Viterbi, I worked with Muhao Chen and Jonathan May. I earned my bachelor’s degree at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I worked with Snigdha Chaturvedi and Colin Raffel. I have also interned at Amazon Alexa AI, IBM Research, and Salesforce AI Research. My research has been reported by MIT Technology Review, and I received the EMNLP 2024 Outstanding Paper Award.
